VTIU LMS

Privacy policy

How VTIU handles information provided through its learning and payment services.

Information we collect

We collect information needed to provide education services, manage accounts, process applications and payments, communicate with users, and maintain system security. This may include identity, contact, academic, transaction, and technical information.

How we use information

Information is used to deliver courses, manage admissions, record academic activity, confirm payments, provide support, prevent fraud, and meet legal or institutional obligations.

Sharing and security

We limit access to authorized staff and service providers who help operate the LMS. Payment details are processed by our payment provider; VTIU does not request or store card PINs or passwords.

Your choices

You may contact info@vtiu.edu.gh to ask about your information, request corrections, or raise a privacy concern.

VTIU payments

Payment and refund policy

Information about payments made through the VTIU LMS.

Payment processing

Payments are processed securely through our payment provider. A transaction is considered received when it is successfully confirmed by the provider and matched to the relevant VTIU record.

Refunds

Refund requests are reviewed by VTIU on a case-by-case basis. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment channel where possible. Processing times may depend on the payment provider and the user's bank or mobile-money service.

How to request a refund

Email info@vtiu.edu.gh with your name, payment reference, amount, date, and reason for the request. Do not send passwords, PINs, or full card details.

Payment support

For a failed, duplicated, or unrecognized payment, contact us promptly with the transaction reference so we can investigate.
